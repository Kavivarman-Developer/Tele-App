Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Application: Hi Talk (TeleApp CRM)

Provider: okboz.co.in

Hi Talk is a telecalling CRM application designed for sales and telecalling teams to manage lead follow-ups, trigger phone calls, and automatically record telecaller performance.

1. Information We Collect and Sensitive Permissions

To provide core telecalling CRM functionalities, our application requests access to the following permissions:

  • READ_CALL_LOG: We access call logs strictly to measure call outcomes (e.g. call connected, missed, duration) for leads assigned to telecalling agents. This data is recorded inside the company CRM lead history so managers and agents can track lead progress. We do NOT read, share, or upload personal call logs unrelated to CRM business leads.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE & READ_PHONE_NUMBERS: Used to detect ongoing call state (ringing, offhook, idle) in order to prompt telecallers to log call notes immediately after a customer call finishes.
  • CALL_PHONE: Used to allow agents to tap and initiate outgoing phone calls to assigned leads directly from within the app.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to alert telecallers about scheduled follow-ups, callback reminders, and newly assigned leads.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing CRM lead management and telecalling workflows.
  • Automating call duration logging and follow-up reminders.
  • Generating telecaller productivity and campaign reporting.

3. Data Protection and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or call log information to any third parties, advertisers, or external marketing networks. All communication between the app and our server is encrypted via SSL/HTTPS.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Lead and call activity records are retained securely as long as the organization's account is active. Users or account administrators may request permanent deletion of their account and associated data by contacting our support team.
